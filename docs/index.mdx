[CRCON](https://github.com/MarechJ/hll_rcon_tool) gives your Hell Let
Loose server a full web admin UI: live game view, player management,
bans, VIPs, automod, Discord webhooks, and a public scoreboard.

This template runs it on [Railway](https://railway.com): no VPS, no Docker,
no config files.

All you need is your game server's

- **IP address**
- **RCON port**
- **RCON password**

Simply click the "Deploy on Railway" button below, and follow the steps.

[![Deploy on Railway](https://railway.com/button.svg)](https://railway.com/new/template/hll-crcon-cloud)

## Four steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Fill in the three values and hit Deploy">
    The button above opens Railway. Only the **backend** service asks
    for anything: your game server's IP, RCON port, and RCON password.
    Then click **Deploy**.

    <Frame caption="The only three values the template asks for">
      <img src="/images/railway-config.png" alt="Railway deploy screen asking for HLL_HOST, HLL_PORT and HLL_PASSWORD" />
    </Frame>

    <Warning>
      The RCON port is its own port, not the game or query port. Wrong
      port or password is the number one reason for an empty dashboard.
    </Warning>

  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for the services to come online">
    Railway builds and starts all seven services. This normally takes
    around 30 seconds; if one sits in "Queued" for a while, that's
    Railway's build queue and it sorts itself out. The backend logging
    `Waiting for database migrations` is normal too; it starts by
    itself once the database is ready.

    <Frame caption="First boot complete: everything Online">
      <img src="/images/railway-project-online.png" alt="Railway project with all seven CRCON services online" />
    </Frame>

  </Step>
  <Step title="Find your two web addresses">
    Click the **frontend** service, open **Settings**, and scroll to
    **Networking**. Your deployment has two addresses, made for you
    automatically:

    - The **Port 80** address is your **admin UI**: this is where you
      log in and manage your server.
    - The **Port 81** address is your **public scoreboard**: share this
      one with your community.

    <Frame caption="Your two addresses, under the frontend service's Settings">
      <img src="/images/railway-two-domains.png" alt="Railway networking settings showing two domains, one on port 80 and one on port 81" />
    </Frame>

  </Step>
  <Step title="Log in and change the password">
    Open the **Port 80** address and sign in with `admin` / `admin`.
    Then **change that password immediately** in the admin UI.

    <Frame caption="Your CRCON login page">
      <img src="/images/crcon-login.jpg" alt="CRCON sign-in page" />
    </Frame>

  </Step>
</Steps>

That's it. Your server name, live map, and player list appear on the
dashboard within a minute.

<Accordion title="About the public scoreboard">
  The scoreboard address shows data once you turn the scoreboard on inside the
  CRCON admin UI. Playing Hell Let Loose: Vietnam? Also change `HLL_GAME` from
  `hll` to `hllv` on the backend service.
</Accordion>
