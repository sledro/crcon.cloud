Stuck on something? Here's where to go, in order.

## 1. Check the troubleshooting page

Most first-boot worries are covered in
[Troubleshooting](/guides/troubleshooting), including the log messages
that look scary but are completely normal.

## 2. Ask on the template's help board

For anything about deploying or running this template, post on our
help board on Railway. That's where we answer questions, and it's the
fastest way to reach us:

<Card
  title="Get template help"
  icon="circle-question"
  href="https://station.railway.com/all-templates/e1fae538-feb1-4274-9888-27767d75fc38"
>
  Open a help thread for this template on Railway Station. Include what you
  clicked, what you expected, and what happened instead.
</Card>

## 3. Questions about CRCON itself

For CRCON features (automod rules, scoreboard settings, Discord
webhooks, and so on) the tool's own community is the expert:

- The [CRCON wiki](https://github.com/MarechJ/hll_rcon_tool/wiki)
- The [CRCON Discord](https://discord.gg/hRS2tKH)
